    Academic & curriculum sources

    Standard undergraduate texts in Physical Education, Sports Science, Kinesiology and Biomechanics, plus NCERT / CBSE and UGC-prescribed PHE and B.P.Ed syllabus frameworks used to organise the study modules.

    How figures are stated

    Court and field dimensions follow the international code of the governing body named in the article; where codes differ (FIBA vs NBA, FIVB indoor vs beach, etc.) the variant is stated in the text. Always verify against your institution's prescribed rulebook before an exam.
